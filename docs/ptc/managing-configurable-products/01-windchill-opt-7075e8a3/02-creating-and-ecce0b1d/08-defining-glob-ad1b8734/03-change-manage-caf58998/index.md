# Change Management of Choice Rules

> 来源：PTC Windchill Help Center（R13.1.2.0）  
> 原文：[PTC Help Center](https://support.ptc.com/help/windchill/r13.1.2.0/en/Windchill_Help_Center/options/OptionsChangeManageRules.html)  
> 获取日期：2026-11-12

<a id="wwID0E5CAR"></a>

<a id="wwID0EODAR"></a>

Choice rules follow the change management process as other Windchill objects. Choice rules can be included in promotion requests. You can access the change management information and actions on the **Changes** tab of the information page of a choice rule. On the rule information page, click Actions > New, and then select the appropriate change management action. Alternatively, you can access the change management actions from the Option Pool or Option Set page, by right-clicking a choice rule in the choice rule table in the right pane, and then selecting the appropriate action.

<a id="wwID0EIEAR"></a>

Out-of-the box, choice rules have a life cycle template with the standard states: In Work, Released, and Canceled.
