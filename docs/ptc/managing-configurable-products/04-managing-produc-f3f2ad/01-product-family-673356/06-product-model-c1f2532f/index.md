# Product Model Group and Product Model Information Pages

> 来源：PTC Windchill Help Center（R13.1.2.0）  
> 原文：[PTC Help Center](https://support.ptc.com/help/windchill/r13.1.2.0/en/Windchill_Help_Center/prodfamily/ProdFamilyProdModelInfoPage.html)  
> 获取日期：2026-11-12

<a id="wwID0EQNZT"></a>

<a id="wwID0EBOZT"></a>

The product model group and product model information pages are a central location for viewing object and system attribute information about a product model or product model group.

<a id="wwID0EGOZT"></a>

The Product Family, to which a product model or product model group belongs, is indicated in the **Attributes** table.

<a id="wwID0EQOZT"></a>

For product models, the information page also lists the configurable module and option set that are assigned to the model, or are inherited from the Product Family structure.
