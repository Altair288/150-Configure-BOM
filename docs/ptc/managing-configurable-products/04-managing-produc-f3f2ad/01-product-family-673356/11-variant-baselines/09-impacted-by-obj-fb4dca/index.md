The **Impacted By Objects** table can be configured to display on the information page of a baseline. It lists the change tasks that impact the baseline. For more information on the Impact Association feature, see [About Impact Association](https://support.ptc.com/help/windchill/r13.1.2.0/en/Windchill_Help_Center/changemanagement/ChgMgmtAbtImpAssoc.html).
